CognitiveMaps++ · switchyard · interdependence ladder

Six rungs of the switchyard, each with a playable shortest path

Every rung is the same 7×7 four-room yard with one more coupling mechanism switched on. Each card shows a real generated map for that rung and animates the BFS-optimal solution from a start state (solid) to a goal state (dashed ghost). Press play, or step through and watch gates flip, crates get pushed, plates held, chutes entered.

workercrate gate 0gate 1gate 2 plate (dots = gates it holds open)chute (arrow = only allowed entry direction) wall

Levers are drawn as small handles carrying the colours of the gates they toggle. A closed gate is a solid bar across its doorway; an open gate is a thin outline. State bits shown top-right of the canvas.
