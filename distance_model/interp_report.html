Latent Walks

Does the recall-flow integrator's latent walk mean anything? Three probes on freshly retrained image checkpoints (683 maps / 167k pairs and 600 / 201k, L5 unseen-maps split): the walk length is the distance, the walk decomposes by entity, and it detours through the states that enable the task.

01The walk length is the distance

Both models share the recurrence; only the readout differs. The integrator is supervised through Σ‖Δz‖, the decode head reads distance off the final tokens and leaves its walk unsupervised. If the walk were an epiphenomenon, both walks would look the same.

02The walk decomposes by entity

Each held-out pair gets a BFS-optimal path decomposed into worker moves, crate pushes, and lever pulls; each entity gets the slot that attends to its cell. The honest statistic is the residual correlation: per-slot cost against per-entity moves after regressing total distance out of both sides, which kills the null where every slot just tracks the total.

03The walk visits the enabling states

A linear decoder (worker-slot token at t=0 → grid cell, trained on held-in maps) reads a worker position out of every intermediate pass. Pairs are split by ground truth: pulls required (the no-pull graph cannot reach the goal, or only slower) vs a pull-free optimal path exists. Question: does the decoded transient put mass on lever cells exactly when the task needs a lever?

The within-pair control is the sobering half: on pairs pulling a strict subset of the map's levers, mass on the pulled lever beats the other levers only 42–60% of the time. Same verdict as the factored-env study: the routing is spatial, not a step-by-step re-enactment. The transient reliably visits the enabling region; at T=4 it only weakly commits to which lever.

Provenance. The original campaign never saved weights, so all checkpoints are same-seed retrainings on A40s. Same-arch determinism is exact (a control run reproduced the recorded 0.952 corr / 1.321 MAE to the last digit, and the sym baseline replicated 0.789 exactly); cross-arch retraining drifts by the run-to-run noise floor, which is why idv_map_s1 reads 0.904 here vs 0.963 recorded and decode-head s0 swung to 0.684, its documented instability.
Method notes. 4096 held-out pairs per model, per-pass slot tokens dumped via --loadckpt --dumptraj; analysis in traj_switchyard.py (DELPI, branch image-based-experiments). BFS path decomposition validated against the distance labels on 100% of pairs. Pull-required includes goals unreachable without pulls (most of them: goal gate-bits differ from start). Decoder is a 49-way linear probe, trained t=0 on 70% of maps, evaluated on the rest; it is trained unseeded, so accuracy wobbles a little between analysis runs. T=4 gives four latent steps: temporal-ordering claims are out of reach by design.

04Night shift

Overnight probes on the same checkpoints plus a fresh wire-split one; verified findings only, negatives included.

The walk accumulates, it does not park

Running a T=4 checkpoint for more passes keeps adding cost roughly linearly: corr degrades gently (idv 0.78 / 0.86 / 0.90 / 0.89 / 0.85 at T = 1 / 2 / 4 / 8 / 16) while MAE explodes (9.7 / 6.9 / 1.9 / 9.8 / 28.0). Ranking survives extrapolation, calibration lives at the trained T. This is the opposite of the factored recall-flow, which parked at the goal and extrapolated budget-free. Removing goal re-injection at inference costs 0.09–0.20 corr: recall is load-bearing but not catastrophic.

The decoded walk leaves the start and follows the route

Decoded worker position per pass: start-cell mass decays monotonically (int600_s3: 0.74 → 0.57 → 0.33 → 0.20 → 0.14) while goal-cell mass rises weakly (0.007 → 0.033, chance ≈ 0.02). Transient mass sits on the true worker-path cells at 2–3× a matched random-cell control (win fraction 0.61–0.73); decode-head s0 is at 0.20, anti-route. Caveat: the decoder is trained on t=0 tokens, later passes are out-of-distribution for it.

Negatives and structure

Plate visitation: negative. Crate-slot mass on the plate cell is ≈0 on press-requiring pairs (n only 11–26). Visitation is a worker/lever phenomenon here. Geometry: integrator walks curve (net/gross 0.75–0.79), decode-head walks are straighter jumps (0.86–0.89); at T=16 the extrapolated passes march in a fixed direction (direction cosine → 0.98), matching the runaway MAE. Calibration: one worker move costs 0.081–0.085 latent units in all three integrators; a crate push ≈ 0.45; a lever pull 0.79–1.43. The coupling-carrying moves are charged 5–17×. Gates: the decoded transient discriminates which gates within a map: 0.056 on gates the true path crosses vs 0.002 on the others (27×, n=636, best-decoded model). Gates lie on the route, so route-following buys gate identity for free; lever identity would need plan knowledge, and indeed the lever-visit timing shows no consistent temporal order (real plans pull levers early; the decoded visit peaks mid-to-late or smears).

Double dissociation, then the stale-wiring reveal

A fresh rewired-causality checkpoint (0.909) keeps walk = distance (0.907) but loses the decomposition (lever residual 0.135) and its lever visitation looks reversed. Cross-evaluating each checkpoint on the other split's pool dissociates the two mechanisms cleanly, with accuracy transferring both ways (0.916 / 0.910): decomposition is a model property (map-trained keeps lever residual ≈0.5 on any pool, wire-trained lacks it on any pool), while lever visitation is a split property (both models show it on unseen layouts, both lose it on rewired-familiar layouts).

The stale-wiring probe explains why. On rewired pools, restricted to pairs where the training wiring and the current wiring demand disjoint levers, the decoded transient puts 19–41× more mass on the stale levers (correct under the training wiring: 0.037 / 0.059) than on the currently correct ones (0.002 / 0.001). The walk re-enacts the remembered plan for that layout while the distance output stays accurate: routing is a memory, and the distance readout does not depend on visiting the right levers. Caveats: n=45 strict pairs per model, win fraction 0.42–0.47 (means driven by strong cases), wire-model decoder acc 0.26, one seed per model.
