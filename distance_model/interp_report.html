Latent Walks

Does the recall-flow integrator's latent walk mean anything? Three probes on freshly retrained image checkpoints (683 maps / 167k pairs and 600 / 201k, L5 unseen-maps split): the walk length is the distance, the walk decomposes by entity, and it detours through the states that enable the task.

01The walk length is the distance

Both models share the recurrence; only the readout differs. The integrator is supervised through Σ‖Δz‖, the decode head reads distance off the final tokens and leaves its walk unsupervised. If the walk were an epiphenomenon, both walks would look the same.

02The walk decomposes by entity

Each held-out pair gets a BFS-optimal path decomposed into worker moves, crate pushes, and lever pulls; each entity gets the slot that attends to its cell. The honest statistic is the residual correlation: per-slot cost against per-entity moves after regressing total distance out of both sides, which kills the null where every slot just tracks the total.

The lever slots, the carriers of the environment's interdependence, decompose cleanly in every integrator and in no decode-head trunk. This is stronger than expected: the earlier factored-env study put the image clean-index at 0.24, and here the coupling-critical entity separates anyway.

03The walk visits the enabling states

A linear decoder (worker-slot token at t=0 → grid cell, trained on held-in maps) reads a worker position out of every intermediate pass. Pairs are split by ground truth: pulls required (the no-pull graph cannot reach the goal, or only slower) vs a pull-free optimal path exists. Question: does the decoded transient put mass on lever cells exactly when the task needs a lever?

The within-pair control is the sobering half: on pairs pulling a strict subset of the map's levers, mass on the pulled lever beats the other levers only 42–60% of the time. Same verdict as the factored-env study: the routing is spatial, not a step-by-step re-enactment. The transient reliably visits the enabling region; at T=4 it only weakly commits to which lever.

Provenance. The original campaign never saved weights, so all checkpoints are same-seed retrainings on A40s. Same-arch determinism is exact (a control run reproduced the recorded 0.952 corr / 1.321 MAE to the last digit, and the sym baseline replicated 0.789 exactly); cross-arch retraining drifts by the run-to-run noise floor, which is why idv_map_s1 reads 0.904 here vs 0.963 recorded and decode-head s0 swung to 0.684, its documented instability.
Method notes. 4096 held-out pairs per model, per-pass slot tokens dumped via --loadckpt --dumptraj; analysis in traj_switchyard.py (DELPI, branch image-based-experiments). BFS path decomposition validated against the distance labels on 100% of pairs. Pull-required includes goals unreachable without pulls (most of them: goal gate-bits differ from start). Decoder is a 49-way linear probe, trained t=0 on 70% of maps, evaluated on the rest; it is trained unseeded, so accuracy wobbles a little between analysis runs. T=4 gives four latent steps: temporal-ordering claims are out of reach by design.
