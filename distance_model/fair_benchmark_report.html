Where the integrator actually wins: a fair-benchmark ledger

Twelve hours, ~230 runs on CIIRC A40s, dgx-osu and the OSU workstations. Every baseline retuned (depth × lr, nan-guarded, same encoder / data / loss / steps), our model rerun at its own validated recipe, seeds everywhere. Verdict: the recall-flow integrator's edge is real but narrow, and it lives in one place: generalizing to unseen coupling structure between factors under weak entity binding. It has no edge on length extrapolation or unseen-map spatial reading.

METHOD FINDINGSWhat changed the numbers, and what didn't

  • Baselines were two-layer-locked. Sym/Qmet/Scalar ignored --layers; IQE's best is 4 layers, and unlocking it alone moved IQE wire 0.70 → 0.77. Now --baselayers, --heads, --gradclip, non-finite-step guard (nskip), and every hyper-parameter is in the RESULT json.
  • The “layernorm bug” is real and inverted: a LayerNorm on the latent before the metric head hurts IQE (0.77 → 0.56) and MRN. It is a swept knob, off in every table above.
  • Our own drop was self-inflicted. The first fair pass ran the integrator at lr 1e-3 + gradclip (chosen on an 8k screen). Verbatim replication of the original command on current code reproduces 0.827/0.807/0.785 map and 0.850/0.827 wire exactly; lr 1e-3 alone costs ≈0.07. No code regression.
  • MRN is fragile: diverges at d256, on crateworld links2/dofhi (marker), and on 2/3 switchyard seeds; when it trains it is competitive. Gradclip does not save it (the spike is in the forward pass).
  • Binding is the CNN bottleneck: supervised binding aux lifts the integrator 0.79 → 0.885/0.861 on switchyard. Worth an unsupervised route (temporal consistency, not plain reconstruction).
  • Recall depth is a weak lever in-range: T2 0.76 → T4 0.77/0.82 → T14 0.79/0.78 → T20 0.81/0.84 (seed 0) but T20 seed 2 collapses to 0.71/0.69. Not a reliable gain.
  • Run-to-run noise ≈ 0.02–0.03 at fixed seed on the image beds. Differences under 0.03 are not results.
what this means for the story

The defensible claim is narrow: on unseen coupling structure between factors, and increasingly so as entity binding gets harder, the accumulator generalizes better than tuned quasimetric / symmetric / scalar heads (crateworld combo/links2 +0.06–0.08 on marker, +0.02 on CNN; switchyard wire +0.04). Length extrapolation and unseen-map reading are not where it wins. A new environment should amplify held-out coupling structure with rendered (not symbolic) dependencies.
