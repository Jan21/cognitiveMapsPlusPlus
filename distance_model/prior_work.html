Distance as Accumulated Latent Displacement

Has anyone computed a goal-conditioned value function as the summed per-step displacement norms of a recurrent network's own hidden trajectory? Four independent deep-research runs (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok), 2026-08-11. Raw reports in distance_model/raw/.

Verdict

All four reports independently reach the same conclusion: no exact precedent exists. The specific mechanism, reading a goal-conditioned distance out as the accumulated per-step latent displacement norm of the network's own recurrent trajectory, does not appear in the surveyed literature across goal-conditioned RL, quasimetric learning, iterative reasoning networks, latent-space geometry, PDE-based distance fields, or computational neuroscience.

The consensus qualification: the method is partially novel, strongly novel on the readout. The target quantity (shortest-path distance as value function), the substrate (weight-shared recurrent transformer), and even the re-injection trick (Bansal et al.'s "recall") are all established. The quantity Σ‖Δz‖ itself also appears repeatedly, but always in a non-output role: as a regularizer, a diagnostic, or a halting signal. Using it as the supervised prediction target is the crux of the novelty.

A second, arguably stronger novelty surfaced by two reports independently: budget-free extrapolation. The entire iterative-reasoning literature extrapolates by running more iterations on harder problems. Here the step count T is fixed and the distance magnitude is carried by per-step increment size. ChatGPT's slogan: "distance magnitude is carried by latent velocity rather than computational duration," and it rates this inversion as possibly a bigger conceptual contribution than the sum-of-norms formula itself.

The mechanism under test

From integ_distance.py: a shared encoder maps state s and goal g to token sets. One weight-shared transformer block runs for T recurrent steps; goal and start tokens are re-injected every step (recall). No scalar head, no embedding-pair norm. The prediction is the arc length of the latent trajectory itself:

cost += Σᵢ ‖zᵢ(t+1) − zᵢ(t)‖₂ for t = 1..T; D(s,g) = softplus(scale) · cost

Training supervises only ground-truth BFS geodesic distances (in some settings only local, short-range pairs); evaluation tests extrapolation to longer distances and unseen environments. No policy is learned; the network is a pure distance oracle.

One non-obvious property (ChatGPT): because the norm is taken before the sum, this is not the telescoping identity z(T) − z(0) = Σ Δz. Turns in the latent trajectory do not cancel; the readout measures path length, not displacement.

Why the claim holds up: five discriminating lenses

Each report converged on the same way of separating the method from its neighbors. These lenses are the skeleton of a related-work section.

1. Refine vs. sum
Iterative networks (VIN, GPPN, Deep Thinking, Universal Transformers, DEQ, recurrent-depth LLMs) refine an answer and read it from the final state. This method sums a quantity across the iterations. DEQ is the sharpest contrast: it deliberately discards the trajectory and keeps only the fixed point.
2. The role of Σ‖Δz‖ (Claude's taxonomy)
Where the quantity does appear, it is a regularizer (Neural ODE kinetic energy, OT-Flow transport cost), a diagnostic (trajectory-length expressivity measures, "Truth as a Trajectory"), or a halting signal (recurrent-depth transformer exit criteria). Never the supervised output. The contribution can be pitched as promoting a known regularizer/diagnostic to the readout.
3. The summation axis
Plenty of methods sum local costs, but over the wrong index: environment or replay-graph paths (Search on the Replay Buffer), explicit graph edges (Bellman-Ford GNNs, NBFNet, Neural A*), an optimized geodesic curve (Riemannian latent geometry), or ODE time on a fixed flow (OT-Flow). Here the index runs over computational depth: the network's own inference trajectory.
4. Who produces the curve
Riemannian latent-space methods integrate speed along a curve found by a separate per-pair geodesic solve on a frozen generator. Here the network is simultaneously the metric and the curve producer, amortized in one forward pass.
5. Velocity vs. duration
Deep Thinking, Huginn, and neural algorithmic reasoners need more iterations for larger instances; drift-diffusion models accumulate to a bound and encode the answer in stopping time. This method is the inverse on both counts: fixed horizon, magnitude in the increments. Doubling the predicted distance does not double compute.

Computes per-step ‖Δz‖ and step angles in recurrent-depth transformers and builds an exit criterion that halts when updates stop changing.

∫‖v‖² dt over the flow's own compute steps: the exact accumulated object, differentiated through during training.

Quasimetric RL

Same problem, same local-to-global philosophy, and the strongest architectural bias toward valid distances. All read distance from a static embedding pair in one shot. Note a structural difference that cuts both ways: these architectures guarantee the triangle inequality; the accumulated-increment readout does not, since each (s,g) query induces its own latent flow.

Eikonal and PDE distance fields

Distance emerges from local consistency (‖∇T‖ = 1/v) enforced as a loss on a feedforward field. Gemini's framing: our architecture is a discrete analogue of Eikonal line integration (ds = ‖Δz‖) implemented by token flow instead of PDE residual minimization. Two recent works already marry Eikonal constraints with GCRL, so this community is moving toward the same territory from the PDE side.

Threats to the novelty claim

Do not claim (each is blocked by specific prior art):

  • "First to represent distance as a latent path length": blocked by the Riemannian latent-geometry line.
  • "First to sum hidden-state displacements across network depth": blocked by "Truth as a Trajectory" and the trajectory-length diagnostics.
  • "We introduce recall in iterative networks": directly conflicts with Bansal et al. 2022.
  • The IQE/MRN reviewer attack. IQE sums nonnegative components over latent dimensions; MRN sums two architectural terms. A skeptical reviewer will call the step-wise accumulation "a temporal generalization" of these. Prepare an explicit articulation of why summing across compute steps differs materially (the increments are produced sequentially by a goal-conditioned flow and support magnitude-based extrapolation), plus head-to-head baselines.
  • Triangle inequality is not guaranteed. Each (s,g) query induces its own latent flow, so nothing enforces d(a,c) ≤ d(a,b) + d(b,c) across queries, unlike PQE/IQE/DeepNorm/MRN where it is architectural. Report it honestly; it doubles as the structural difference from the quasimetric family.
  • Unverified recent preprints. The most mechanically relevant items are 2025-2026 preprints read partly from abstracts (arXiv:2509.23314, "Truth as a Trajectory", temporal-distance JEPA work, looped-transformer halting papers). Claude's falsification criterion: if any of them sums per-step displacements into a supervised distance or value output, downgrade the claim from "novel readout" to "partial anticipation" and cite it as the primary baseline. Read the primary texts, not the abstracts.
  • Unsearched communities. Neural survival analysis (cumulative hazard), amortized geodesic solvers, action-functional learning, sub-Riemannian metrics: same scalar under different names may exist there.

How to frame the contribution

Merged recommendation across reports: claim the readout, not the task; present it as a new parameterization of goal-conditioned distance, not a new notion of distance, a new recurrent architecture, or a new latent path integral.

ChatGPT's suggested claim sentence, worth adopting nearly verbatim:

"Prior goal-conditioned distance models read values directly from state-goal embeddings or scalar value heads, while iterative planners and recurrent reasoning networks read their predictions from the final recurrent state. In contrast, we parameterize the goal-conditioned distance as the discrete arc length of the model's own recurrent latent inference trajectory, summing nonnegative hidden-state displacement magnitudes across a fixed number of weight-shared iterations."

Structure related work along two axes: (i) accumulation-as-output vs. regularizer / diagnostic / halting / final-state; (ii) fixed-T magnitude-based extrapolation vs. iteration-count-based extrapolation.

Baselines the paper needs (Claude, explicit): head-to-head against IQE, MRN, and a scalar-head regressor, showing the increment-sum readout extrapolates to longer distances and unseen environments where they do not. This preempts the "why not just a quasimetric embedding?" review.

Must-cite-and-distinguish set: QRL + IQE; Bansal et al. (recall); Schwarzschild et al.; Arvanitidis et al.; OT-Flow / Finlay et al.; arXiv:2509.23314; "Truth as a Trajectory"; and for the evaluation claim, Horizon Generalization (Myers et al. 2025).
