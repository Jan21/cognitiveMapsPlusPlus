Pure-image scoreboard: integrator vs tuned baselines, rung by rung

Input = rendered start and goal images only (walls, entities, gate bits, lever→gate wiring, plate, chute all as pixels; no symbolic tokens). Encoder = per-pixel 1×1 MLP → 8 or 12 learned slots by one-shot cross-attention. Same encoder, data, loss and 80k steps for every head; baselines at their on-bed tuned depth × lr; 3 seeds per bar (dots = seeds). Metric = Pearson r between predicted and true BFS distance on held-out maps.

Levels: L0 maze · L1 +push · L2 +gates (1 lever = 1 gate) · L3 +XOR wiring · L4 +plate · L5 +chute (full) · L6 4 gates / 3 levers. Integrator uses 12 slots at L3–L6 (8 at L0–L2, where 8 ≥ #entities). Baselines: on-bed tuned configs at L3–L6, defaults at L0–L2 (tuning moved them ≤0.04).

2 · REWIRED CAUSALITYWire split: same layouts, lever→gate and plate→gate wiring resampled at test

Integrator with 12 slots at all rungs; baselines at on-bed tuned configs. Reading which lever now drives which gate, purely from pixels, is where the integrator separates most and every seed beats every baseline seed at L2–L5.

3 · MARGIN VS COUPLINGIntegrator minus best tuned baseline, per rung

Three curves: pure image / map, pure image / wire, and the symbolic (factored-token) ladder as the perception-free reference. Symbolic: margin ≈0 at L0–L1 → +0.10–0.11 at L3–L5. Image: same shape, damped (+0.02 → +0.05–0.07), because perception still costs ~0.1 at L3+.

5 · WHAT FIXED PERCEPTIONPinpointing the L1→L2 break (40k, unseen maps)

L1½ = static gate bits, no levers; L1¾ = one gate + one lever. The 3×3 CNN loses ~0.1 the moment gate bits must be read per cell (blur across neighbours); the 1×1 per-pixel encoder recovers to the symbolic reference once the optimizer is tamed (lr 1e-3, grad-clip, warmup — the bars here are the pre-fix recipe, hence the bimodal seeds).

6 · SLOTS: WHAT HELPED, WHAT DIDN'TBinding experiments on the current best model (L3 / L5, 80k)

Slots ≥ entities (12) is the one clear win (+0.06 at L3). Reconstruction aux and attention-entropy sharpening hurt on this encoder; 16 slots ≈ 12. Supervised Hungarian ceiling still running.
Competitive iterative slot attention (softmax over slots, GRU, 1 or 3 iterations, ± reconstruction) vs one-shot cross-attention slots, identical safe recipe, 80k, 3 seeds. One-shot wins at every rung; the competitive variants plateau (train MAE ≈ 4) or overfit. IQE on the same competitive tokens is shown for scale.

7 · HONEST SUMMARY

what holds

From pixels only, against tuned IQE / symmetric / scalar heads with 3 seeds, the recall-flow integrator is the best model at every rung of both splits. Margins are small on unseen maps (+0.02 to +0.07) and larger and seed-separated on rewired causality (+0.04 to +0.07, all seeds above all baseline seeds at L2–L5). The symbolic ladder shows the undamped version of the same curve (+0.10 at full coupling).

what does not

Length extrapolation, unseen-map spatial reading in isolation, DOF extrapolation: no edge (earlier ledger). Perception still costs ~0.1 at L3+ vs symbolic; competitive slot attention, reconstruction aux, entropy sharpening, hard attention, wire-path rendering, more maps: all tried, none helped on this encoder. Only "1×1 encoder + safe optimizer + slots ≥ entities" moved the needle.
