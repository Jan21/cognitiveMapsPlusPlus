Headline

The large bed, everyone re-tuned

683 training-split maps with the pair budget scaled to match (167k pairs). Same data, loss, steps and objectness plane for every model.

Two readings. First, the margin does not shrink when baselines get more data and a fresh tuning pass; it grows slightly. Second, the scalar head, which collapsed to 0.73 on the lean bed, revives to 0.839 here, but with a ±0.06 seed spread (one seed reached 0.884, another landed near 0.77). The monolithic readout is data-hungry and unstable; the quasimetric and embedding heads are stable but plateau lower.

Two levers

Map diversity alone does nothing; diversity plus pairs does everything

The jump to 0.953 initially looked like a map-diversity effect. It is not. Splitting the levers:

With the pair budget frozen, spreading it over 3.4× more layouts moves nobody: every model within noise, margins unchanged. The gain appears only when supervision pairs scale with the layouts, and it accrues mostly to the integrator: its train error stays low while transfer improves sharply, so the extra data is buying perception, not metric capacity. Longer training on the large bed (120k steps) adds nothing.

Best checkpoint: every run on the large bed logged a held-out evaluation curve (--evalevery). Best-on-curve exceeds the final score by at most +0.01 corr for every model, so checkpoint selection plays no role in any number here.

Readout

The decode head, resolved

Same joint recurrence, two ways to read a distance out of it: the integrator's accumulation (D = softplus(s)·Σ&lVert;Δz&rVert;) versus an MLP decode of the final tokens. The decode head looked broken on images; the investigation found one real implementation gap on our side (a missing output softplus, now fixed to match the reference code) and then, with the implementation validated by symbolic controls, a clean pattern:

The decode head is also optimization-fragile: at lr 2e-3 it collapses to a dead softplus (constant output) on both beds, and lr 1e-3 collapses the same way even on the large image bed; the reference tie itself ran per-readout learning rates (accumulation at 2e-3, decode at 1e-3). On the lean image bed no screened recipe (lr 3e-4 to 2e-3, warm-up, T 1/4/8, cosine, ±softplus) lifts it past 0.753, and every one underfits. Scaling data helps the decode head (0.753 → 0.813) but helps the accumulation more, so the gap widens rather than closes: under learned perception the deficit is not data hunger. A dedicated decode-head recipe screen on the large bed (8 further configurations) is running; the ladder above is the standing result.

Audit trail

Fairness checks that ran before these numbers

  • fixedDecode head lacked the reference's output softplus. Added; softplus does not rescue it on images (lr 1e-3 nan, lr 5e-4 → 0.70–0.73), and symbolic controls at the right lr validate the implementation (0.841 where accumulation gets 0.904).
  • no effectScalar baseline given the same softplus parity (reference concat_mlp has it): flat on its flatten variant (0.719 vs 0.721), worse and underfitting on its slot variant (0.682 vs 0.730). Off in all tables. IQE, MRN and sym are nonnegative by construction.
  • fixedThe first 683-map comparison ran baselines at 49k pairs against an integrator trained on 167k: the pair-budget flag was not logged in results, which hid the mismatch. Now logged; every screen rerun at the matched bed.
  • cleanCode bisect after the discrepancy: old file, current file, eval-curve on, eval-curve off are bit-identical in training (0.866 / 2.373 at fixed seed). The instrumentation added for best-checkpoint tracking has zero training effect.
  • checkedWinner configs rerun without the objectness plane: IQE, sym and MRN within ±0.01; scalar needs it (0.755 vs 0.884, seed 0). The integrator without it: 0.906 map / 0.928 wire (2 seeds) — the plane is worth +0.047 map at scale (vs +0.017 at 200 maps), yet the plain-pixels integrator still leads every re-tuned baseline by +0.05 / +0.09.

Method in one paragraph

Full switchyard (7×7, crate, 3 gates, 2 levers with hidden wiring, pressure plate, one-way chute), pure image observations: 12 binary stencils plus an objectness plane, no symbols, no hand-built detector. Distance supervision from BFS. Splits: unseen maps (held-out layouts with their own wiring) and rewired causality (same layouts, wiring resampled). All models share encoder family options, training data, loss and step budget; baselines additionally searched over encoder, pooling, depth, width and lr on each bed they are reported on. Beds: 200 maps / 49k pairs, 683 / 49k, 683 / 167k. 3 seeds everywhere unless noted. Raw per-seed numbers and screen top-5s: paper_data/ablations_raw.json; prose: paper_data/ablations.md.
