The distance-model campaign: every experiment, every result

Consolidated report, 2026-08-15 morning. Covers the Switchyard benchmark campaign, the length-generalization investigation across three environments, the replication audit of the historical extrapolation results, and the ten-mechanism exploration of fixes. Roughly 120 training runs across five machines (A40 cluster, two DGXs, V100 node, consumer GPUs). All numbers are test MAE (lower better), with correlation where stated; "beyond" = MAE on distances past the training range.

The campaign's three headline findings:

  1. The interdependence advantage is real, seed-stable, and grows with both coupling depth and data scale. On Switchyard, the accumulated-displacement readout beats a matched scalar head by a margin that rises monotonically along the coupling ladder (0.05 → 1.38) and with map count (best: 1.55 vs 2.78 at 1600 maps). The symmetric-embedding baseline is worst of the three on every Switchyard split.
  2. Length extrapolation is environment-dominated, and the historical "budget-free extrapolation" survives replication only on its home environment, where an under-trained-baselines caveat now applies. On coupled worlds, every readout (ours, scalar, symmetric, with/without globalbase and parking) fails in the same band.
  3. The fix exploration found two mechanism families that break the failure band: scale-routing (route magnitude through components that extrapolate by construction: residual-over-proxy 4.7, linear-mix head 4.1-4.5, both tightly replicated) and self-consistency (Bellman bootstrap: reaches 2.25, the best coupled-world extrapolation ever recorded here, but converges unreliably; a target-network stabilization is the identified next step). Walking-based, output-space, and sparse-composition mechanisms are eliminated.

The corrected historical claim: "budget-free extrapolation" replicates as a phenomenon on guarded product graphs (flat to ~2x trained range, parking required for long budgets), but does not support a superiority claim (a plain transformer trained equally long is 10-20x better there) and does not transfer to any coupled world tested, under any recipe, including the exact original one.

Eliminated explanations (controls run)

  • Learning rate (1e-3 vs 2e-3), budget size (T=14/24/32), training length (40k/80k): tested, insufficient.
  • --globalbase (the base-constant fix from earlier probes): inert on both coupled worlds.
  • Parking (arrival loss + anytime budgets + long test budgets): 10/10 negative on coupled worlds, while its home-turf control confirms the mechanism works there.
  • Failure signature everywhere: within-range precision and local move-ranking stay intact; predictions saturate at the trained range. The flow is an amortized jumper, not a walker: nothing scales past supervision.

Part III: the ten-mechanism fix exploration

All on the coupling world, train ≤8 → test 25 (the 3.1x protocol), identical model and training otherwise; plain control replicated at 10.6-12.2. Sorted by outcome:

Mechanism families, interpreted

  • Scale-routing (works, stable): route magnitude through components that extrapolate by construction. resprox (predict d − exact factorized proxy): 4.7-4.8 with within-range 0.02-0.06, best in-range accuracy ever recorded on the protocol. linmix (linear head over flow cost + coordinate-gap features): 4.1-4.5, keeps local ranking healthier.
  • Self-consistency (works when it converges): 1-step Bellman bootstrap on unlabeled pairs reaches 2.25/corr 0.79 at 3.1x and 2.71 at 2.1x, but spreads 2.3-9.4 across seeds/weights. Multi-step random-walk targets make it worse, not better.
  • Walking-based (eliminated): soft step-counting, step caps, proxy-scaled budgets, parking: the flow is an amortized jumper; no penalty made it walk.
  • Output-space (eliminated): log-space regression reshapes saturation without curing it; globalbase inert.
  • Sparse composition (eliminated as implemented): 128 random waypoints cannot form trusted chains in a 150K-state space; would need trajectory-derived waypoint sets to test fairly.

Status: still in flight

  • Bellman confirmation battery: w=1.0 seeds 1-2, w=1.5, and combos bellman+resprox, bellman+linmix, bellman+walks (the decisive stability test).
  • Exploration stragglers: bounds, distill, kitchen-sink combo, remaining seeds (cluster queue contended).
